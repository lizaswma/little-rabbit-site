Little Rabbit Timer Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

The short version: Little Rabbit Timer doesn't collect, store or share any information about you or your child. Everything the app needs stays on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code. It never connects to the internet, so nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To work, the app saves a few settings on your iPhone or iPad:

  • The custom timer length you last chose
  • Which scene Little Rabbit acts out for each timer
  • The end time of a running timer, so the countdown continues if the app closes

These settings are shared only with the app's own Home Screen widget and Lock Screen countdown. They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

The app asks to schedule alarms (on iOS 26 and later) or to send notifications (on earlier versions) so the timer can ring when it finishes, even if the app is closed. iOS handles these alarms on your device. You can turn them off any time in Settings.

Children's privacy

Little Rabbit Timer is made for families with young children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. This is consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated before the new version is released, and the date above will change.
